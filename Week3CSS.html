Company Profile

Ncell was established in 2004 and began operating as Mero Mobile in 2005. Rebranded to Ncell in 2010, it now provides 2G, 3G, 4G, and is ready for 5G services across Nepal.

We are constantly working towards the goal of connecting every Nepali through our network, providing high-quality modern services to consistently create better value.
